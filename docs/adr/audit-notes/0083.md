## Harvested: On mechanizing this ADR

*Verbatim from the ADR body. The rewrite deletes the original; this is the copy.*

Decisions 1–3 all have direct test coverage. Two arms would close the gaps this
audit found:

- **The claim's blind spot.** Make `_advance_unit_body_clock` refuse to claim a
  unit that is a member of a live `CombatLoop.units`, or assert after
  `_hand_off_clocks_to_combat` that every member reports `COMBAT`. The second is
  a one-line addition to `NavigatorLiveCombatDoublePumpTest`, which already
  asserts both stand-ins are `COMBAT` after the handoff (`:129`) — it just does
  not exercise a unit whose setter no-ops.
- **`tick_based` has no setter.** A text arm asserting no `.gd` assigns
  `tick_based` locks in the fold that makes the bad state unrepresentable. It
  passes today and is the cheapest of the three.
