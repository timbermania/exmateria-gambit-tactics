## The recorded question is answered, not carried

The previous audit left "retitle, or keep the title as history?" open, reading it
as accuracy *versus* citations. The corpus already separates the two: **63 of 187
ADRs have an H1 that is not the filename slug**, and `gen_adr_index.py:51` takes
the **H1** as the displayed title while the filename is only the link target. So
the filename is an address and the H1 is the claim. The filename is kept — four
ADRs (0070, 0071, 0073, 0075) link it by name — and the H1 now states the now.

The previous audit's cost estimate was wrong in its membership: it listed
`0071, 0073, 0075, 0085` as the full-filename citers, but 0085 cites `ADR-0069`
bare and **0070** is the fourth filename link.
