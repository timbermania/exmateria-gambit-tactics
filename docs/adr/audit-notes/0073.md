# ADR-0073 — audit notes

*Verified 2026-08-28 and folded the same day. ADR-level verdict: `complete` —
all eleven decisions are built. This file records what the fold deleted, what it
corrected, and the two counts it could not settle.*

## Numbering and retargets

Decs. 1–6 keep their integers. The 2026-08-18 amendment's decisions A–D became
**decs. 7–10**; the 2026-08-21 amendment's single decision became **dec. 11**.
Seventeen citations were retargeted in the same commit — 13 in source, 2 in
tests, 2 in ADRs (0085, 0102) — plus `0103`'s "self-contained-ref amendment",
which now cites decs. 1 and 8 by number.

One of them was **wrong before the fold**: `EffectStudioPage.gd:5579` cited
"ADR-0073 amendment 2026-08-21, **decision 6**". That amendment had no numbered
decisions; decision 6 belongs to ADR-0085's amendment of the same date. It now
cites `ADR-0073 dec. 11`, which is the rule it actually implements.

## Built past what the ADR described

- Dec. 2 said "`span` and `emitter` are BUILT; `frameset/frame/curve/animation/
  callback` are declared seams". The registry now holds **11 kinds, 9 built** —
  `span`, `emitter`, `container`, `pair`, `effect_settings`, `texture`,
  `frameset`, `frame`, `animation` — with `curve` and `callback` the only
  remaining seams. Four of the built kinds (`container`, `pair`,
  `effect_settings`, `texture`) are kinds this ADR never named, added by later
  ADRs. That is dec. 2's own claim holding: adding a kind was a registration.
- Dec. 6 said "the emitter browser". There are **four** exhaustive browsers —
  emitter, container, frameset, sequence.
- A third link carrier shipped and was never written down: a `nav_choice`
  dropdown collapsing several links into one cell
  (`EffectKeyframeInspector.gd:992`). Folded into dec. 3, which owns link
  shapes.
- The trail moved. The 2026-08-21 amendment's consequence named
  `_breadcrumb_rows` suppressing for a chain stack; **no such function exists**.
  The trail is the path bar's (ADR-0102) and the suppression lives in
  `EffectStudioPage._path_crumbs()`, which returns `[]` when `_nav_chain`. The
  rule survived the mechanism; the ADR now states the rule.

## "Build pending" was stale

The 2026-08-21 amendment carried **"Status: design settled, build pending."**
It is built: `_nav_chain`, `_seed_sound_chain()`, `_sections_for_render()`
composing every entry, `_chain_index_of()` → `_inspector.scroll_to_section()`
for the on-screen degradation, and `_latched_editor_h` keyed on `_nav[0]`
(`EffectStudioPage.gd:3089`). `EffectStudioChainInspectionTest` guards it.

## Counts this fold could not settle

The lens-ambiguity measurement says "over all **481** effects" and, four
sentences later, "**383 of 401** effects have a single frameset group". Two
totals for one corpus. The plausible reading is that 481 counts effect files and
401 counts those with animations, but nothing in the tree says so, and no
`E###.BIN` is present in this checkout to re-measure. The ADR now states the
measurement without a corpus total; the discrepancy is recorded here rather than
resolved by picking one.
