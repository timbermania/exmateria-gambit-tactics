# ADR-0101 — audit notes

*Verified 2026-08-28. ADR-level verdict: `complete`, with one open fault counted by the
build's own sweep. Written by the de-amendment rewrite, which consumed both amendments.*

## What the fold did

- Eight decisions kept their numbers — **nothing cited an amendment by name**, and every one
  of the ~60 `ADR-0101` citations in `src/` and `tests/` addresses a `decision N` / `dec. N`,
  so no citation moved. `decisions 3-7` citations stay valid.
- **Amendment (2026-08-19, "the manufactured pad is minted, not copied")** folded into
  **dec. 6**. It narrowed the padding rule and, in doing so, closed the open faults recorded
  under decs. 6 and 7; both open-fault blocks are deleted rather than carried.
- **Amendment (2026-08-20, "the gesture is structure-free")** promoted to **dec. 9** — it is a
  new decision about *when* the splice runs, not a restatement of decs. 6/7's rule, and dec. 3
  no longer claims the per-motion apply path.
- The five "Sharpened while building" blockquotes folded into the decisions they annotate.
  Decision 4's sharpening is the substantive one: `free_slots ≥ 8` is a worst-case **bound**,
  and using it as a **gate** is what made Move one-way. The decision now states the pricing
  rule, not the bound.
- Deleted as spent: the amendment's before→after table, the E-number witness lists, the
  "conservative repair vs faithful repair" deliberation (neither was taken — minting removed
  the question), the address-chase narrative, and the `Status` header's four paragraphs of
  build bulletins.

## Open, and recorded in the register rather than the body

1. **The ONE-WAY census stands at 3**, all one shape and not a padding fault: the *moved span
   itself* folds inert in the position it lands on, so the planner will not address it ("a hold
   is empty space, not a movable span") — E510 `for_each` #0 and #4, on both the palette and the
   screen arm. The general statement: `ColourMovePlan` reasons over a hold vector taken
   **before** the edit, and the edit changes what the fold says.
2. **A minted record's own edge grip can steal the grab back.** `_grip_reach = clamp(tile_w −
   BODY_MIN_W, 0, 4.5)` puts a 9px grip wholly inside a thin pad. Move is immune since dec. 9
   (it mints nothing until release); **insert and delete are not** — they still renumber the
   lane mid-gesture.
3. **Unruled by the author:** when you drag a span back, does the bar get *wider*? Independent
   of everything above, and never decided.

## Guard arm still open

The corpus sweep asserts five invariants plus the round trip, and `ColourMovePadShapeTest`
covers the pad's shape — this ADR is among the best-guarded in the corpus. The arm that is
absent is for item 1: assert that a Move never lands a span in a position where the span itself
folds inert. It would report 3 today and fail, correctly, which is why it is a `proposed_guard`
and not a commit.
