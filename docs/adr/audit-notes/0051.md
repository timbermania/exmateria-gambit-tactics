# ADR-0051 audit notes

Audited 2026-08-28, during the de-amendment pass (one amendment folded, one audit
amendment consumed). Graded against `tools/check_no_env_vars.py`,
`src/scenarios/ScenarioCameraDirector.gd`, `src/world_map/WorldMapScene.gd`,
`src/debug/`, `src/ui3/testing/`, and `tests/run_all_tests.sh`.

## Verdict

`half-landed`. Dec. 2 is mechanized at hard zero and decs. 3–5 are built. Dec. 1 is
**violated** — not by an env read, but by the other half of the trade: three knobs
whose surface was deleted.

## Numbering

Amendment 1 (2026-08-26, #612 — the `--` user-args rule) is promoted to **dec. 5**.
Five citations retargeted in the same commit: `godot-learning/CLAUDE.md:101`,
`docs/WORLD_MAP_PORT_LIST.md:1198`, `src/debug/WorldMapDebugPanel.gd:44`, and the
appended amendments in ADR-0161 and ADR-0174. ADR-0052's
`(ADR-0051, amendment 2026-08-28)` is retargeted to `dec. 1` + issue #677.

## The violation

The 2026-07-04 landing deleted the `SCENARIO_*` camera env vars on the grounds that
they were "now the 'Disable camera takeover' checkbox" on
`src/debug/ScenarioCameraDebugPanel.gd`:

| Date | Commit | Event |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-06-21 | `10e6bca73` | `ScenarioCameraDebugPanel.gd` added — "live-calibrate the 0x19 Camera opcode" |
| 2026-06-24 | `c33d70536` | this ADR accepted, naming that panel as one of three exemplars of dec. 1 |
| 2026-07-04 | `09a126301` | guard lands; the five `SCENARIO_*` camera envs deleted, "now the … checkbox" |
| 2026-07-06 | `137b94b40` | `ScenarioCameraDebugPanel.gd` **deleted**, inside `checkpoint(scenario): … accumulated debug WIP` |

The replacement surface survived the env vars by two days, in a commit whose subject
mentions nothing about it. `ScenarioCameraDebugPanel` now occurs nowhere in the tree.
`camera_disabled`, `camera_backrotate_invert` and `camera_flip_body_depth` have
readers and no writers. Both readings — restore the surface, or close the knobs — are
recorded in **#677**; nothing in the tree records why `137b94b40` dropped the panel.

This is invisible to the guard **by construction**: `check_no_env_vars.py` asserts the
*absence* of env reads and cannot assert the *presence* of the panel whose existence
was the argument for deleting them. A rule that trades one surface for another is only
half-mechanized if only the retired half is checked.

## Claims corrected in the fold

- **"Written nowhere" is false for three of the five knobs.** The prior audit listed
  `camera_backrotate_pivot:88`, `camera_backrotate_invert:94`, `camera_aim_floor_y:140`
  and `camera_vertical_datum:186` together as orphans. Measured: `camera_aim_floor_y`,
  `camera_vertical_datum` and `camera_backrotate_pivot` are written by
  `ScenarioCameraSwoopMonotonicTest`, `ScenarioCameraFloorAimCalibTest`,
  `ScenarioCameraVerticalDatumTest` and `ScenarioChapelChainTraceTest`, which
  parameterise them for A/B assertions. They are live test parameters, not orphans, and
  the "delete the settled knobs" reading is materially weaker than it looked. The real
  orphan set is `camera_disabled`, `camera_backrotate_invert`, and (from ADR-0052's
  audit) `camera_flip_body_depth`.
- **"Eighteen files already take rig parameters this way."** 21 — and 21 at Amendment
  1's own commit (`05eef2d2d`, 2026-08-26), so the number was wrong when written, not
  stale from growth. `src/world_map/WorldMapScene.gd:319` repeats the same 18 (#678).
- **Amendment 1's conversion claim was true of one document of three.** It said the
  `MENU=` / `SHOT=` / `FIXTURE=` / `ZOOM=` spellings "in ADR-0161, ADR-0174 and
  `docs/WORLD_MAP_PORT_LIST.md` now read `-- --menu=…`". Only the port list was
  converted; five body occurrences across ADR-0161 and ADR-0174 still spelled the
  retired form, each sitting *above* the note that corrects it — exactly the failure
  that bullet's own last sentence names ("the env form does not error — it is ignored").
  Converted in this commit.
- **Dec. 1 named a deleted exemplar.** `ScenarioCameraDebugPanel` is replaced in the
  bullet by `CombatUIDebugPanel`, which exists.

## Filed as issues, not fixed here

## Not this ADR's to fix

The Consequence pointed at CLAUDE.md's "Common Mistakes" table. That table no longer
exists under that heading — the entry lives at `docs/pitfalls.md`, and the relocation
also unmoored pointers in ADR-0034, ADR-0052 and ADR-0060. That is a corpus-wide
pointer fix, not this ADR's. The rule itself landed *stronger* than asked:
`godot-learning/CLAUDE.md` bans throwaway debug gating (`*_DIAG`) as well as config
and carries dec. 5.
