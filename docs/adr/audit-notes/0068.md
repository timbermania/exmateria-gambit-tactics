## Four stale claims the fold removes

Every number in the addenda was re-measured rather than trusted. Four did not hold:

1. **"Materialize's `CALLS` map still points at the OLD `Tune.bind` arg positions — the
   only piece deferred."** Not true any more. The table carries the 4-verb API
   (`Tune.bind` `(0,1)`, `Tune.bind_update` `(1,2)`, `TuneField.add` `(2,3)`,
   `TuneField.build_control` `(0,1)`) plus two `TunePort.*` rows added when #588 re-pointed
   26 addon registration lines onto the port façade, and `resolve_static_var_default`
   implements R6's one-hop follower. The deferred piece is built.
2. **R5's "~64 direct `Tune.of` sites remain."** Zero remain; the method does not exist.
   The only occurrences of the name in the tree are comments describing its retirement.
3. **Decision 7's "There are no per-field `persist:` flags in code."** There are — but they
   are the narrow exception, not the norm, and decision 10 already anticipated the case.
   `Tune.Persist` is `{EPHEMERAL, TUNABLE, AUTOSAVE}`; a registration with no declared
   class rests at `TUNABLE`, and the declaration only ratchets up, so a stray registration
   can neither downgrade an `AUTOSAVE` slug nor upgrade an `EPHEMERAL` one. Decision 7 now
   says that. This is a restatement, not a fork: both halves of the original sentence — no
   classification burden, and the pin/commit gesture *is* the persistence decision — survive
   intact for the resting case.
4. **Test tallies.** `TuneTest` 88 → **96**, `TuneFieldTest` 63 → **65**,
   `PilotStaticInitTest` 6 → **3**. The rewrite drops the counts rather than fixing them,
   as ADR-0188's fold did: a raw tally has no design content and drifts on every edit.
   `## Verification` names the suites and what each pins.

## Named but not built → `proposed_guard`

Decision 17 describes four arms; **one** is built (the runtime unconsumed-scrub detector,
`Tune.poll_unconsumed_scrubs`), and the codemod's non-literal warning is a second. Three
preflight arms are not: a bound home that is a `const` with more than one reader; a debug
panel that writes game-node state directly; and "exactly one writer to a static-var home".
Decision 19's tripwire (mark `Tune` as applying, flag any `free()` in that window) is also
unbuilt — the addendum said so itself, under "Enforcement (open)".

`tools/check_debug_panel_tunables.py` is adjacent to but not the same as the second of
those: it flags a raw value widget in a debug panel (is this field routed through
`TuneField` at all?), not a panel writing game state. It also runs in **report mode** — it
prints outstanding sites and exits 0 — so it is a reporter, not yet a gate. The rewrite
states that plainly in `## Verification` rather than letting "guarded by" imply a hard gate.

## No open question

Nothing forced a reading. The one restatement that changes a decision's letter — decision
7's persist classes — is settled by the tree plus decision 10 read together, not by a
choice between two designs. The unbuilt guard arms are recorded rather than decided, and
the two genuinely open items (a committed-override tier, perf at scale) are stated as
not-built and unmeasured in Consequences, which is what they were.
