## Numbers reconciled before quoting

- **`unit_flat.gdshader` is 177 lines, not the 163 Amendment 1 recorded.** 163 is correct
  at `4abd321e5`; `8c471a5d0` (#634, "three stale comments described the world ADR-0077
  abolished") grew it to 177 — 36 lines touched, comments and two dead `render_priority`
  writes, no compositing change. The ADR now states 177 as the current fact.
- **The shared include is 907 lines**, not the 939 the pre-build Context measured.
- **The drift was 22 of 24 byte-identical functions**, not the 19 of 21 the pre-build
  Context stated over a smaller function set. The ADR now carries the measured pair.
- `check_color_shaders.py`'s `REQUIRED_CONSUMERS` is **five** files today (the three unit
  variants, `indexed_color`, `screen_background`) where dec. 7 was written against three.

## Open

- **The flat variant's PAR debt is unpaid.** It is `BURN_DOWN` entry 1 of 11 in
  `check_par_shaders.py`, repathed by dec. 7 rather than converted. Dec. 7 records that an
  exemption is arguable on that guard's own terms and declines to make the call for the
  other ten formation shaders. Not a defect of this ADR; a note for whoever burns that list
  down.
- **`SpriteLayerManager.initialize(anim_set, shader_material)` still takes a material from
  its caller.** Dec. 3 accepts the residual hole deliberately and hands it to dec. 8's
  guard, which catches a *path*, not a hand-built `ShaderMaterial`. A caller could still
  construct one from a `Shader` it obtained some other way. No current caller does.
- **The uniform scan is still blind repo-wide.** `uniform\s+\w+\s+(\w+)` cannot match
  `uniform vec2[10] type1_rects;` and reads 35 where there are 62. `UnitMaterialVariantTest`
  routes around it (deriving 46 names from `unit.tres` plus `SpriteLayerManager`), but the
  regex itself is used elsewhere — tracked separately, not by this ADR.

## Proposed guard

None. Dec. 8's guard plus `UnitMaterialVariantTest`'s two arms cover the seam; the three
open items above are either another guard's debt or out of scope.
