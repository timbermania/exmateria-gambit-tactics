# ADR-0269 — audit notes

## The capture rig cannot judge the band; a lit battlefield can

`tests/TurnQueueHudShot.tscn` draws the strip on the viewport's flat grey. Decision 7's
backdrop is a SUBTRACTIVE `UIVitalsBand` routed through the 4.8 fork's engine fold, and on a
flat grey a subtract is indistinguishable from a painted near-black rectangle — so the rig
cannot tell the shared producer from the cheap `Color(0, 0, 0, 0.55)` alpha quad the decision
chose it over. That is a property of the instrument, not of the build, and it was recorded as
a soft spot when dec. 7 landed.

**Looked at, 2026-09-09.** `godot --path . assets/scenes/GambitBattle.tscn -- --combat-autostart`
over Gariland, screenshotted with a turn open:

- The band subtracts from the SKY and lands as the same dark navy the vitals band at the
  bottom of that same frame lands on. One look, two bands, one producer — the read dec. 7 is
  for.
- Eight framed cards span about 72% of the display width at the top-left, clear of the vitals
  panel and of the nameplate. `max_cards = 8` needs no adjustment on this queue shape.
- The team underlay carries the side at a glance behind each head, without the `Enemy` word
  cell dec. 2 removed.
- Each card's `Frame.MENU_TILE` reads as the same chrome the vitals panel and the nameplate
  wear, which is critique 5's complaint answered.

**Round 3's two asks, and what they cost.** *"can the dark band go alllll the way across the
screen"* and *"instead of fading can they aperture close/open and wait for the one to close
before moving ahead"*. They are the same ticket: a screen-wide band is the bar's own payload, so
widening it widens the bar, and a card that box-opens needs `OWN_APERTURE`, which it cannot have
while the bar is cropping it. The card leaving the bar's subtree answers both.

**Looked at again, 2026-09-09 (round 3).** `tests/TurnQueueHudShot.tscn`, whose mid frames now
print the apertures they caught: settled `band 100% of 373 px, head card 100% of 32 px`; stage
one `60% / 0%`; stage two `100% / 59%`. The band runs edge to edge in the settled shot, and the
card row still starts at the `screen_pos` anchor plus `pad.x` rather than at the band's left
edge, which is the half a merely-wider band would have got wrong.

⚠️ **The two-stage open was FOUND by looking, not by reasoning.** The first build of round 3
opened the band and the cards on the same edge. Both are centre-out box-opens, but the band's
box is 373 display px and a card's is 32: at 60% the band's scissor has reached x=60..283 while
the cards at x=6 and x=38 are already 59% open, so for four frames two framed portraits stand on
the bare battlefield with nothing behind them. Visible immediately in `turnqueue_midopen.png`,
invisible to every assertion the test had. The band now opens first and the cards follow it; the
close is the mirror.

**Still not looked at:** the strip at PAR 1.25. `PSXDisplay._ui_par_default` is 1.0, so every
capture behind this ADR — including this one — is square-PAR, and 1.25 comes back through the
debug scrub. `portrait_base()` is a function of the live PAR, so the layout follows it, but
whether eight cards still fit at PAR 1.25 is arithmetic nobody has run against a frame.

**Still not looked at (round 3):** `turnqueue.band_sub`. The band's strength (the oracle's
`120/255`) was judged over Gariland's sky when the band stopped at the last portrait. There is
now roughly three times as much of it on screen, and how dark a backdrop should be is a looking
question about how much of it there is.
