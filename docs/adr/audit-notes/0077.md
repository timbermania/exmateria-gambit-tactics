## The method finding that this ADR is the case law for

Numbering `## Amendment (2026-08-13)` to `## Amendment 1` was attempted **before** this pass and
reverted: `check_adr_anchors` went red on `tests/DetailUnauthoredDepthTest.gd:3`, which cites
`ADR-0077 Amendment 2026-08-13` — a *dated* anchor, and the only one of its kind in the
twenty-five ADRs audited to that point. The pre-flight grep that had cleared the move four times
before missed it because it was **case-sensitive** and the citation capitalizes "Amendment".

The lesson stands and generalizes past renumbering: sweep **case-insensitively**, and include
`.gd` docstrings, not just `.md` prose. What changed here is that a *fold* does not have the
renumbering problem — it deletes the heading outright, so every citer must be retargeted anyway,
and the sweep above is that retarget.

  `src/ui3/detail/DetailScene.gd`, the sentinel is gone, and both cited lines are unrelated
