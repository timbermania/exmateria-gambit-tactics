## What the amendments were correcting, kept because the method outlives the fix

**Dec. 4's original measurement was wrong in every term.** It read *"only nine of the 29
shaders declaring `compositor_layer` are mechanical … the other twenty carry the producer's
own fragment body."* The true population is **16** (`grep -rlE '^render_mode[^;]*compositor_layer'`
— 31 files *contain* the string; the rest say it in a comment or are the deliberately-unrouted
`effect_native_*` twins). Six are mechanical, being **two** triplets, not three:
`effect_native_{add,mix,sub}` are the non-fold twins and declare nothing. And the argument's
biggest counter-example, `unit_flat.gdshader` at 94 code lines, **is not a fold shader** — its
`render_mode` is `unshaded, cull_disabled` and it names `compositor_layer` only in a comment
about a different mesh. The refusal survives its corrected measurement, but not on the ratio.

**The ADR disagreed with itself for six commits.** Its own post-build census said 16 while
dec. 4 said 29, and the 29 was also in `Fold.gd`'s class docstring and the `0191` row of
`docs/adr/CLASSIFICATION.tsv`. That a census and the decision it was appended to could carry
different denominators that long is the finding: *a census is only a check if something
compares it to the claim.*

**`tests/ShaderCompileTest.gd` was green over wrong arithmetic.** It claimed the 16 split
"10 under `assets/shaders/` and 6 under `src/ui3/shaders/`"; the real split is **6 / 6 / 4**,
the four in `addons/exmateria_battlefield/` being in neither bucket. Its own assertion only
checks that both named roots are non-zero.

**Amendment 2's premise was false for three of the fourteen copies.** The reach was not one
form but three, and only one is tree-dependent:
`get_node_or_null("/root/CompositorAutopilot")` (`UI3Element` — which guarded it —
`UIUnitNameplate`, `DetailScene`, `FormationScene`, plus 5 in `tests/`);
`Engine.get_main_loop().root.get_node_or_null(...)`, which reaches the root without being in
it (`DamageNumber3D`, `EffectCallback`, `TileCursor`, plus 2 copies in
`tools/run_effect_editor_engine_fold.gd`); and `_window.fold_owns()` (`EquipPickerMenu`,
inheriting `UI3Element`'s guard). So three classes answered correctly off-tree and could not
have moved. That reach-form census is what bounds the regression — not the probe.

**And the probe proved less than it was made to say.** Every `Fold.owns()` site in the six
migrated files was rewritten to print when it ran off-tree, and the 112 scenes that statically
reach them were run against it: zero off-tree hits. But zero was also reported at `z_for`'s own
site — the one site independently proven to have moved, because `UI3ElementSpecTest` calls
`add_child` before `z_for`. A negative that also covers the known positive discriminates
nothing. What it establishes is *"no test in that set exercises an off-tree fold ask"*.

**Every census in this ADR was seeded on the wrong noun, twice.** Amendment 3 found
`UIVitalsBand` because the earlier censuses grepped for the *predicate*, and a producer that
*receives* the answer never names it. Amendment 4 §2 hit the same error one level along: the
handoff's census of unconditional `load()`s was 3 sites because its grep was seeded on the
*variable name* (`fold_shader|FOLD_SHADER|fold\.gdshader`), so `MODE_SHADER[mode]`,
`FOLD_ADD/SUB/MIX` and `probe_demi_engine_fold`'s inline literals were invisible. Re-seeded on
the **shaders** — files whose `render_mode` declares `compositor_layer` — the population closes
at 6. Dec. 11's guard is seeded that way for the same reason.

**The site table for dec. 12 did not add up on its first draft.** It said 3 in the re-enrol row
and summed to 14, filing `FormationScene`'s band re-enrol in no row at all. It is 5 + 2 + 4 + 4
= **15**, re-counted here against the tree.

## The 1,965 guard lines are dropped, not restated

Dec. 9 originally read *"the thirteen guards are unchanged — all 1,965 lines of them."* The
figure is **not re-derivable**: nothing in the repo says which thirteen scripts it covers
(`grep -rn '1,965\|1965'` finds only this ADR and its generated index), and the thirteen
shader-scanning guards `run_all_tests.sh` invokes sum to **1,996**. With 40 `tools/check_*.py`
files some 13-subset does hit 1,965 by arithmetic accident — which is the point: an undefined
set makes the figure unfalsifiable, not merely unverified. Dec. 9 now states the claim that is
checkable (none retired, 13 → 14) and drops the one that is not.

## The run tally is dropped

The *Verification* section reported 694/714 with twenty non-passers in three clusters. Two of
the three explanations are claims about *one worktree on one machine* rather than about this
work: the twelve world-map failures were attributed to a `.gitignore`d `assets/world_map/`
absent from that checkout, and four Effect Studio acceptance tests to load on a swapping box.
Both may be true and neither is a property of the decisions. The ADR now names the suites and
guards that hold each decision, which is what a reader needs, and the run stays in the branch's
history where it belongs.
