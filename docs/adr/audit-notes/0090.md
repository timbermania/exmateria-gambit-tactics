## The one substantive finding, folded into decision 3

The accumulator is `delta * TRANSPORT_HZ * _speed() * _pacing_factor_now()`
(`EffectStudioPage.gd`), carrying a factor the ADR named nowhere while the phrase
"30 Hz × speed" appeared three times. Decision 3 now states the full expression and says
*why* the pacing term is load-bearing rather than incidental: the ADR's argument for
collapsing to one transport path is that a seeked frame equals a free-run frame
(ADR-0070), and a free-run frame is time-modulated, so a page tick ignoring pacing would
advance at a different rate than the same effect playing normally — reintroducing the
"works free-running, subtly wrong looping" bug class the collapse exists to remove.

## Two corrections to Amendment 2, which the rewrite does not carry forward

Amendment 2's own grading was accurate clause by clause against the tree — re-measured,
not trusted — with two exceptions:

1. **It attributed the pacing factor to "ADR-0014's pacing curve."** The code comment
   beside the accumulator credits `#270 / ADR-0093`, and the two ADRs are different
   things: ADR-0014 is *EffectTimeline owns time modulation outright* (the runtime
   `_time_scale_factor` the page mirrors) and ADR-0093 is *time-scale pacing curves are
   freehand painted* (the authored curve `EffectScoreModel.pacing_factor_at` reads). The
   rewritten decision 3 cites both, in those roles.
2. **Its guard table listed six suites; the tree has eight.** It omitted
   `tests/EffectFramesBarRegionTest.gd` (decision 1's Alt+left-drag gesture) and
   `tests/EffectStudioTransportTest.gd` (decision 3 at the page). `## Verification` names
   all eight, plus `tests/EffectScoreModelTest.gd`'s `pacing_factor_at` arm, which is what
   covers the newly-stated pacing term.

## No open question

Nothing here forced a reading. Amendment 2 recorded no ambiguity, and the rewrite found
none: every clause of decisions 1–4 has a literal in the tree, and the only claim no suite
can reach — that ping-pong on a long or late-in-timeline region runs below real-time — is
a performance observation the ADR accepts as a limitation rather than asserting as a
bound. Guarding it would need a wall-clock arm this corpus has no place for, so no
`proposed_guard` is recorded.
