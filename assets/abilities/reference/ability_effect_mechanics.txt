\x1b[38;5;208mOVERVIEW\x1b[0m
========

This document is the mechanical companion to ability_semantics.json. Where the
semantics file says "Applies Wall" or "Inflicts Poison," this reference explains
what Wall and Poison actually DO — exact formulas, durations, interactions, and
edge cases.

Covers all 368 Normal abilities across 16 semantic categories:
  - Status Effects (buffs, debuffs, cancellation)
  - Damage (physical, magic, percentage, current/lost HP)
  - Healing (MA-based, percentage, HP+MP, self-sacrifice)
  - Absorb (HP/MP drain)
  - Break / Steal (equipment, gil, exp)
  - Stat Modification (permanent boosts/reductions)
  - Songs / Dances (continuous tick effects)
  - Special (Quick, Golem, level change, CT manipulation)

Data sources:
  - Formula dispatch table at 0x8018f614 (101 function pointers)
  - FORMULA_SEMANTICS from formula_xy_table.py
  - INFLICT_STATUS_NAMES from seed_ability_semantics.py
  - ability_attributes.json (368 entries)
  - PSX BATTLE.BIN disassembly


================================================================================
\x1b[38;5;208m1. STATUS EFFECTS\x1b[0m
================================================================================

Status effects are applied via the inflict_status field (IDs 32-63 for named
statuses, 59-121 for compound sets). Hit-only formulas (0x0A, 0x0B, 0x22,
0x38, etc.) apply statuses without dealing damage.

\x1b[38;5;250mFaith Scaling on Status Hit Rate\x1b[0m
---------------------------------

Formulas marked "F" scale hit chance by Faith:

    Effective Hit% = (MA + X) * (CasterFaith/100) * (TargetFaith/100)

Formulas marked "NS" (Not Signed/Not Faith) skip Faith scaling:

    Effective Hit% = MA + X    (or SP + X for physical formulas)

This means Innocent (Faith=0) targets are immune to Faith-scaled statuses but
NOT immune to NS statuses like Break/Steal.


\x1b[38;5;118m1A. BUFF STATUSES (Positive)\x1b[0m
-----------------------------

\x1b[38;5;228mRaise (ID 32)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Revives a KO'd unit
    Mechanic:   Applied alongside a percentage HP heal (e.g., Raise = Heal 50%
                + Raise status; Raise 2 = Heal 100% + Raise status)
    Duration:   Instant (consumed on application)
    Used by:    Raise (formula 0x0D, y=50), Raise 2 (formula 0x0D, y=100)

\x1b[38;5;228mReraise (ID 33)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Auto-revive when unit is KO'd
    Mechanic:   When unit's HP reaches 0, automatically revives with a small
                amount of HP. Status is consumed on trigger (one-time use).
    Duration:   Permanent until triggered or dispelled
    Cancels:    —
    Used by:    Reraise (formula 0x0B), Angel Ring (innate)

\x1b[38;5;228mRegen (ID 34)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Periodic HP recovery
    Mechanic:   Heals 1/8 of unit's max HP each CT tick
    Duration:   Permanent (until removed)
    Cancels:    Poison (mutual cancellation)
    Used by:    Regen (formula 0x0B)

\x1b[38;5;228mProtect (ID 35)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Reduces incoming physical damage
    Mechanic:   Attacker's PA is reduced by 2/3 (multiplied by 2/3) in the
                physical damage calculation
    Duration:   32 CT ticks
    Cancels:    —
    Used by:    Protect (formula 0x0B), Protect 2 (formula 0x0B)

\x1b[38;5;228mShell (ID 36)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Reduces incoming magic damage
    Mechanic:   Attacker's MA is reduced by 2/3 (multiplied by 2/3) in the
                magic damage calculation
    Duration:   32 CT ticks
    Cancels:    —
    Used by:    Shell (formula 0x0B), Shell 2 (formula 0x0B)

\x1b[38;5;228mWall (ID 37)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Protect + Shell combined
    Mechanic:   Both physical (PA * 2/3) and magical (MA * 2/3) damage
                reduction active simultaneously
    Duration:   32 CT ticks
    Cancels:    —
    Used by:    Wall (formula 0x0B)

\x1b[38;5;228mHaste (ID 42)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Faster turn frequency
    Mechanic:   CT accumulation rate multiplied by 1.5 (gains 50% more CT per
                tick, effectively taking turns ~50% more often)
    Duration:   32 CT ticks
    Cancels:    Slow (mutual cancellation)
    Used by:    Haste (formula 0x0A), Haste 2 (formula 0x0A)

\x1b[38;5;228mFloat (ID 46)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Levitation above terrain
    Mechanic:   Unit hovers above ground. Immune to terrain-based damage and
                ground-targeting effects. Can traverse water/lava tiles safely.
    Duration:   Permanent (until removed)
    Cancels:    —
    Used by:    Float (formula 0x0B)

\x1b[38;5;228mReflect (ID 47)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Bounces targeted spells
    Mechanic:   Single-target spells aimed at this unit bounce to a random
                enemy. Does NOT affect: area-of-effect spells, summon magic,
                Arithmetick-targeted spells, or self-targeted abilities.
    Duration:   32 CT ticks
    Cancels:    —
    Used by:    Reflect (formula 0x0B)

\x1b[38;5;228mFaith (ID 49)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Amplifies magical interactions
    Mechanic:   Sets unit's effective Faith to a high value. Increases both
                magic damage dealt AND magic damage/healing received. Since
                Faith scaling is (CasterFaith/100) * (TargetFaith/100), high
                Faith makes the unit both a stronger mage and more vulnerable
                to magic.
    Duration:   Permanent (until removed)
    Cancels:    Innocent (mutual cancellation)
    Used by:    Faith (formula 0x0A)


\x1b[38;5;118m1B. DEBUFF STATUSES (Negative)\x1b[0m
-------------------------------

\x1b[38;5;228mPoison (ID 39)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Periodic HP loss
    Mechanic:   Deals 1/8 of unit's max HP as damage each CT tick
    Duration:   36 CT ticks
    Cancels:    Regen (mutual cancellation)
    Used by:    Poison (formula 0x0A), Bio series (compound sets)

\x1b[38;5;228mFrog (ID 40)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Transformation — severely limits actions
    Mechanic:   Unit can only use basic Attack and the Frog spell. All stats
                are effectively halved. Frog casting Frog on a Frog cures it.
    Duration:   Permanent (until cured)
    Cancels:    —
    Used by:    Frog (formula 0x0A)

\x1b[38;5;228mSlow (ID 43)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Reduced turn frequency
    Mechanic:   CT accumulation rate multiplied by 0.5 (gains half CT per tick,
                effectively taking turns ~50% less often)
    Duration:   32 CT ticks
    Cancels:    Haste (mutual cancellation)
    Used by:    Slow (formula 0x0A), Slow 2 (formula 0x0A)

\x1b[38;5;228mStop (ID 44)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Complete time freeze
    Mechanic:   CT frozen at current value (no accumulation). Unit cannot act,
                move, evade, or trigger reaction abilities. Unlike Sleep, not
                broken by taking damage.
    Duration:   20 CT ticks (shorter than most timed statuses)
    Cancels:    —
    Used by:    Stop (formula 0x0A)

\x1b[38;5;228mDon't Move (ID 45)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Movement disabled
    Mechanic:   Unit cannot reposition on the battlefield. Can still perform
                actions (attack, cast spells, use items) from current tile.
    Duration:   Permanent (until cured)
    Cancels:    —
    Used by:    Don't Move (formula 0x0A)

\x1b[38;5;228mDarkness (ID 48)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Impaired physical accuracy
    Mechanic:   Physical attack hit rate is halved. Does not affect magic hit
                rates or magic damage.
    Duration:   Permanent (until cured)
    Cancels:    —
    Used by:    Blind (formula 0x0A)

\x1b[38;5;228mInnocent (ID 50)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Complete magic immunity (both ways)
    Mechanic:   Sets effective Faith to 0. Since magic damage/healing scales by
                (TargetFaith/100), this makes ALL Faith-scaled formulas deal 0
                damage and 0 healing. The unit can neither benefit from nor be
                harmed by magic.
    Duration:   Permanent (until cured)
    Cancels:    Faith (mutual cancellation)
    Used by:    Innocent (formula 0x0A)

\x1b[38;5;228mUndead (ID 51)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Inverted healing/damage
    Mechanic:   Healing effects deal damage instead. Damage effects heal
                instead. Phoenix Down / Raise instantly KOs. Cure spells deal
                their heal amount as damage. Potions deal damage.
    Duration:   Permanent (until cured)
    Cancels:    —
    Used by:    Zombie (formula 0x0A), various monster abilities

\x1b[38;5;228mSilence (ID 52)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Spellcasting disabled
    Mechanic:   Cannot use abilities flagged as "not_silence" in ability
                attributes (most magic spells). Can still use physical
                abilities, items, and abilities not flagged.
    Duration:   Permanent (until cured)
    Cancels:    —
    Used by:    Silence Song (formula 0x0A)

\x1b[38;5;228mBerserk (ID 53)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Uncontrollable — auto-attacks
    Mechanic:   Unit becomes AI-controlled. Automatically moves toward and
                attacks the nearest enemy with basic Attack. Receives a PA
                bonus (PA * 4/3). Cannot use abilities, items, or move
                strategically.
    Duration:   Permanent (until cured)
    Cancels:    —
    Used by:    Berserk (formula 0x0A)

\x1b[38;5;228mConfusion (ID 54)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Random targeting — attacks allies
    Mechanic:   Unit becomes AI-controlled with randomized target selection.
                Will attack allies and enemies indiscriminately. Broken by
                taking physical damage from an ally.
    Duration:   Permanent (until cured or broken by damage)
    Cancels:    —
    Used by:    Confuse (formula 0x0A), Befuddle

\x1b[38;5;228mDon't Act (ID 56)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Actions disabled
    Mechanic:   Cannot perform any action (attack, cast, use items). Can still
                move to reposition. CT continues to accumulate normally.
    Duration:   24 CT ticks
    Cancels:    —
    Used by:    Don't Act (formula 0x0A)

\x1b[38;5;228mSleep (ID 57)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Unconscious — vulnerable
    Mechanic:   No CT accumulation. Cannot act, move, evade, or react. Physical
                attacks against sleeping units deal 1.5x damage. Woken by any
                damage (including poison ticks).
    Duration:   60 CT ticks (longest timed status)
    Cancels:    —
    Used by:    Sleep (formula 0x0A)

\x1b[38;5;228mPetrify (ID 58)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Complete petrification
    Mechanic:   Unit is completely frozen. No CT gain, no actions, no reactions,
                no evasion. Treated as KO for battle-end conditions (if all
                non-petrified allies are KO'd, battle is lost). Immune to all
                other status changes while petrified.
    Duration:   Permanent (until cured)
    Cancels:    —
    Used by:    Petrify (formula 0x0A), Break series

\x1b[38;5;228mDeath Sentence (ID 61)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Countdown to death
    Mechanic:   A 3-tick countdown appears above the unit. When it reaches 0,
                the unit is instantly KO'd. Can be cured before countdown
                expires.
    Duration:   3 CT ticks until KO
    Cancels:    —
    Used by:    Death Sentence (formula 0x0A)

\x1b[38;5;228mCharm (ID 62)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Temporary allegiance switch
    Mechanic:   Unit fights for the opposing side. AI-controlled, targets
                former allies. Only works on opposite-sex targets (formula
                0x29: OppositeSex hit check). Broken by taking physical damage.
    Duration:   Permanent (until cured or broken)
    Cancels:    —
    Used by:    Charm (formula 0x29)


\x1b[38;5;118m1C. SPECIAL STATUS OPERATIONS\x1b[0m
------------------------------

\x1b[38;5;228mCancel Statuses / Esuna (ID 38)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Removes negative statuses from target
    Mechanic:   Clears a predefined set of negative status flags. Does NOT
                remove all statuses — only those in the cancellation bitmask.
    Used by:    Esuna (formula 0x0B), Stigma Magic, Heal

\x1b[38;5;228mDispel (ID 55)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Removes positive statuses from target
    Mechanic:   Strips buff statuses: Protect, Shell, Haste, Regen, Reraise,
                Float, Reflect, Wall, Faith.
    Used by:    Dispel Magic (formula 0x0A)

\x1b[38;5;228mDeath+Stop+DontMove+DontAct (ID 41)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Compound instant-kill or total disable
    Mechanic:   Attempts to apply Death first. If Death is resisted, falls
                through to Stop, Don't Move, and Don't Act. Result depends
                on target's immunities.
    Used by:    Galaxy Stop (formula 0x0A), various monster abilities

\x1b[38;5;228mInvite (ID 63)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Permanent recruitment
    Mechanic:   Target permanently joins the player's party. Removes unit from
                enemy roster. Only works on human-type units (not monsters in
                most cases). Battle continues with remaining enemies.
    Used by:    Invitation (formula 0x0A)


\x1b[38;5;118m1D. COMPOUND STATUS SETS (IDs 59-121)\x1b[0m
---------------------------------------

inflict_status IDs 59 and above map to compound status sets defined in a lookup
table in BATTLE.BIN (not fully decoded in this codebase). Each compound set ID
resolves to a bitmask of individual status flags.

Known compound sets:

    ID    Name/Context          Likely Statuses
    ---   -------------------   ----------------------------------------
    59    NamelessSong set      Random positive status (one of the buffs)
    60    NamelessDance set     Random negative status (one of the debuffs)
    85    BloodSuck set         HP drain + status combination
    121   Various monster       Multiple debuff combination

These compound sets are applied by monster abilities, special skills, and the
Nameless Song/Dance random effects. The exact bitmask mapping requires further
reverse engineering of the BATTLE.BIN status set table.


================================================================================
\x1b[38;5;208m2. DAMAGE SYSTEM\x1b[0m
================================================================================

All damage flows through the formula dispatch table at 0x8018f614. The
formula_id (byte in ability attributes) selects one of 101 function pointers.

\x1b[38;5;250mDamage Pipeline\x1b[0m
-----------------

    1. Formula function calculates raw damage from stats + X/Y params
    2. Faith scaling applied (if formula is Faith-type "F")
    3. Element interaction applied (absorb/weak/null/half)
    4. Protect/Shell reduction applied (if applicable)
    5. Random variance applied (~12.5% range)
    6. Final damage subtracted from target HP

\x1b[38;5;250mFaith Scaling\x1b[0m
---------------

Formulas marked "F" (Faith-dependent) multiply damage by:

    FaithMult = (CasterFaith / 100) * (TargetFaith / 100)

Example: Caster Faith=70, Target Faith=50:
    FaithMult = 0.70 * 0.50 = 0.35 (only 35% of base damage)

Formulas marked "NS" (Not Signed / Not Faith) skip this multiplier entirely.
This is why:
  - Innocent (Faith=0) gives complete magic immunity (0 * anything = 0)
  - High Faith units deal AND receive more magic damage
  - Physical formulas (NS) ignore Faith completely

\x1b[38;5;250mElement Interaction\x1b[0m
---------------------

Elements: Fire, Ice, Lightning, Wind, Water, Earth, Holy, Dark

    Target absorbs element  →  Damage becomes healing (negative damage)
    Target weak to element  →  Damage * 2
    Target nulls element    →  Damage = 0
    Target halves element   →  Damage / 2
    No affinity             →  Normal damage

Equipment and innate abilities set element affinities. Multiple elements on a
single ability are possible but rare.


\x1b[38;5;118m2A. WEAPON-BASED PHYSICAL DAMAGE\x1b[0m
----------------------------------

Formulas 0x00-0x05 — standard weapon attacks.

    0x00: Dmg = PA * WP                    (standard melee/ranged)
    0x01: Dmg = PA * WP                    (variant)
    0x02: Dmg = PA * WP (NS)              (ignores Sign/Faith)
    0x03: Dmg = WP * WP (NS)             (weapon power squared, guns)
    0x04: Dmg = MA * WP                    (magic guns)
    0x05: Dmg = PA * WP                    (variant)

Affected by: Protect (PA * 2/3), Brave (for knight swords, katanas)
Not affected by: Faith, Shell, Elements (unless weapon is elemental)

    0x06: AbsHP = weapon damage            (Blood Sword — drains HP)
    0x07: Heal  = weapon damage            (healing rod weapons)

X and Y parameters are unused for all weapon formulas.


\x1b[38;5;118m2B. MA-BASED MAGIC DAMAGE\x1b[0m
---------------------------

The most common damage formula family (41+ abilities).

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x08: Dmg_F(MA*Y)\x1b[0m  — Standard magic damage
    Damage = MA * Y * (CasterFaith/100) * (TargetFaith/100)
    Used by: Fire (Y=14), Fire 2 (Y=18), Fire 3 (Y=24), Ice, Bolt, etc.
    Element: Set per ability (Fire=128, Ice=64, Bolt=32, etc.)

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x20: Dmg_(MA*Y)\x1b[0m  — Non-Faith magic damage
    Damage = MA * Y
    No Faith scaling. Used by: Summon magic (Ifrit Y=28, Shiva Y=24, etc.)

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x4E: Dmg_(MA*Y)\x1b[0m  — Monster magic damage
    Damage = MA * Y
    Used by: Various monster abilities (25 abilities)

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x24: Dmg_((PA+Y)/2*MA)\x1b[0m  — Hybrid damage
    Damage = ((PA + Y) / 2) * MA
    Scales off both PA and MA. Used by: Holy Sword abilities

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x31: Dmg_((PA+Y)/2*PA)\x1b[0m  — PA-hybrid damage
    Damage = ((PA + Y) / 2) * PA
    Used by: Various physical-styled damage abilities

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x1E: Dmg_((MA+Y)*MA/2) multi-hit\x1b[0m
    Damage per hit = ((MA + Y) * MA) / 2
    Number of hits = Random(1, X)
    Used by: Repeated magic attacks (randomized hit count)

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x1F: Dmg with inverse Faith\x1b[0m
    Damage = ((100-CasterFaith) * (100-TargetFaith) * (MA+Y) * MA) / 2
    Hits = Random(1, X)
    Used by: Dark-element abilities that benefit from LOW Faith

\x1b[38;5;228mFormulas 0x5E/0x5F/0x60: Dmg_((MA+Y)/2*MA) variants\x1b[0m
    0x5E: Multi-hit (X+1 hits) with status application
    0x5F: Single hit
    0x60: Single hit, non-elemental, with status
    Used by: High-tier monster/special abilities


\x1b[38;5;118m2C. PERCENTAGE DAMAGE\x1b[0m
-----------------------

Damage based on target's max or current HP rather than caster stats.

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x09: Dmg_(Y/100)% Hit_F(MA+X)%\x1b[0m
    Damage = (Y / 100) * TargetMaxHP
    Hit rate = (MA + X) * Faith scaling
    Used by: Gravity (Y=25), Demi (Y=50), Demi 2 (Y=75)

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x0E: Dmg_(Y)% Hit_F(MA+X)%\x1b[0m
    Damage = Y% of TargetMaxHP
    Used by: Various percentage damage abilities

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x53: Dmg_(Y)% Hit_(MA+X)%\x1b[0m
    Damage = Y% of TargetMaxHP (no Faith on damage, Faith on hit)
    Used by: Monster percentage damage

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x1B: DmgMP_(Y)% Hit_F(MA+X)%\x1b[0m
    MP Damage = Y% of TargetMaxMP
    Targets MP instead of HP

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x2C: DmgMP_(Y)% Hit_(PA+Y)%\x1b[0m
    MP Damage = Y% of TargetMaxMP
    Hit rate uses PA instead of MA


\x1b[38;5;118m2D. CURRENT/LOST HP DAMAGE\x1b[0m
----------------------------

Damage based on HP values rather than stats or percentages.

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x16: DmgMP_(TarCurMP)\x1b[0m
    Damage = Target's current MP (drains all MP, deals as damage to MP)
    Hit rate = (MA + X) * Faith scaling

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x17: Dmg_(TarCurHP-1)\x1b[0m
    Damage = Target's current HP - 1 (reduces target to exactly 1 HP)
    Hit rate = (MA + X) * Faith scaling

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x3E: Dmg_(TarCurHP-1) 100% hit\x1b[0m
    Same as 0x17 but with guaranteed hit (no Faith check)

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x43: Dmg_(CasMaxHP-CasCurHP)\x1b[0m
    Damage = Caster's MaxHP - Caster's CurrentHP (caster's lost HP)
    More damage when caster is injured. Used by: Darkness (Dark Knight)

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x4F: Dmg_(CasMaxHP-CasCurHP) Hit_(MA+X)%\x1b[0m
    Same as 0x43 with MA-based hit rate. Used by: monster abilities

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x52: Dmg_(CasMaxHP-CasCurHP) + status\x1b[0m
    Lost HP damage with 100% status application

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x44: Dmg_(TarCurMP)\x1b[0m
    Damage = Target's current MP (targets HP pool using MP value)

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x45: Dmg_(TarMaxHP-TarCurHP)\x1b[0m
    Damage = Target's missing HP. More effective against injured targets.


\x1b[38;5;118m2E. PA-BASED PHYSICAL DAMAGE\x1b[0m
------------------------------

Non-weapon physical formulas using PA directly.

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x2D: Dmg_(PA*(WP+Y)) + 100% status\x1b[0m
    Damage = PA * (WP + Y)
    Status applied with 100% hit rate (no evasion check)
    Used by: Crush Punch, Holy Explosion, other knight sword techs

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x42: Dmg_(PA*Y) with self-damage\x1b[0m
    Damage = PA * Y
    Self damage = PA * Y / X (caster takes a fraction)
    Used by: Sacrifice-type abilities

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x32: Dmg_(Rdm(1..X)*(PA*3+Y)) NS NE\x1b[0m
    Damage = Random(1, X) * (PA*3 + Y)
    Non-elemental, non-Faith. Used by: random physical attacks

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x37: Dmg_(Rdm(1..Y)*PA) NS NE\x1b[0m
    Damage = Random(1, Y) * PA
    Non-elemental, non-Faith. Used by: monster physical abilities

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x63: Dmg_(SP*WP)\x1b[0m
    Damage = Speed * WP
    Used by: Speed-based attacks (Ninja abilities)

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x64: Jump Dmg_(PA*WP)\x1b[0m
    Damage = PA * WP
    Jump-specific formula (shares handler with 0x63). Bypasses evasion.


================================================================================
\x1b[38;5;208m3. HEALING SYSTEM\x1b[0m
================================================================================

Healing formulas restore HP (and sometimes MP). Affected by Undead status
(healing deals damage instead) and Faith scaling (for F-type formulas).


\x1b[38;5;118m3A. MA-BASED HEALING\x1b[0m
----------------------

\x1b[38;5;228mFormulas 0x0C, 0x23, 0x4C: Heal_F(MA*Y) NS\x1b[0m
    Heal = MA * Y
    0x0C: Standard (Cure=14, Cure 2=18, Cure 3=24, Cure 4=30)
    0x23: Variant (used by summon heals like Fairy Y=36)
    0x4C: Variant (used by monster heal abilities)
    "NS" = not affected by Sign, meaning always heals (not damage)
    Faith scaling: YES — (CasterFaith/100) * (TargetFaith/100)

    Example: Cure 3, MA=12, CasterFaith=70, TargetFaith=60:
        Heal = 12 * 24 * 0.70 * 0.60 = 121 HP


\x1b[38;5;118m3B. PERCENTAGE HEALING\x1b[0m
------------------------

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x0D: Heal_(Y)% Hit_F(MA+X)%\x1b[0m
    Heal = Y% of target's max HP
    Used by: Raise (Y=50), Raise 2 (Y=100), Chakra variants

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x35: Heal_(Y)% Hit_(PA+X)%\x1b[0m
    Heal = Y% of target's max HP
    Uses PA for hit rate instead of MA (physical-based healer)


\x1b[38;5;118m3C. HP+MP HEALING\x1b[0m
--------------------

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x34: Heal_(PA*Y) HealMP_(PA*Y/2) NS\x1b[0m
    HP Heal = PA * Y
    MP Heal = PA * Y / 2
    Used by: Chakra (heals both HP and MP in one action)


\x1b[38;5;118m3D. MP-ONLY HEALING\x1b[0m
----------------------

================================================================================
\x1b[38;5;208m5. BREAK & STEAL\x1b[0m
================================================================================

Break and Steal formulas target equipped items. The equipment slot is
hardcoded per ability_id in FUN_801879c8.


\x1b[38;5;118m5A. EQUIPMENT BREAK\x1b[0m
----------------------

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x25: Break Hit_(SP+X)%\x1b[0m
    Permanently destroys the target's equipped item in a specific slot.
    Hit rate = Speed + X (NOT PA+WP+X as some wikis claim — PSX code at
    FUN_80185e30 uses Speed)
    Slot determined by ability_id hardcoded mapping:
        HeadBreak  → helmet slot
        ArmorBreak → armor slot
        ShieldBreak → shield slot
        WeaponBreak → weapon slot

    The item is removed from the target permanently. Cannot break items
    flagged as unbreakable. Used by: Knight "Break" abilities (8 total)

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x2E: Break + Dmg_(PA*WP) NS\x1b[0m
    Deals PA * WP physical damage AND breaks equipment
    Combines damage with equipment destruction


\x1b[38;5;118m5B. EQUIPMENT STEAL\x1b[0m
----------------------

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x26: Steal Hit_(SP+X)%\x1b[0m
    Transfers target's equipped item to caster's inventory.
    Hit rate = Speed + X
    Slot determined by ability_id hardcoded mapping:
        StealHelm → helmet slot
        StealArmor → armor slot
        StealShield → shield slot
        StealWeapon → weapon slot
        StealAccessry → accessory slot

    Cannot steal items flagged as un-stealable.
    Used by: Thief "Steal" abilities (5 equipment types)


\x1b[38;5;118m5C. GIL AND EXP STEAL\x1b[0m
-----------------------

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x27: StealGil_(CasLVL*SP) Hit_(SP+X)% NS\x1b[0m
    Gil stolen = Caster's Level * Caster's Speed
    Hit rate = Speed + X
    Used by: StealGil

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x28: StealExp_(min(TarExp,SP+Y)) Hit_(SP+X)%\x1b[0m
    Exp stolen = minimum of (Target's Exp, Speed + Y)
    Hit rate = Speed + X
    Used by: StealExp


================================================================================
\x1b[38;5;208m6. STAT MODIFICATION\x1b[0m
================================================================================

Stat modifications are permanent changes to a unit's base stats (not timed
buffs). They persist for the rest of the battle.


\x1b[38;5;118m6A. STAT BOOSTS\x1b[0m
-----------------

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x36: +PA_(Y) NS\x1b[0m
    Permanently increases target's PA by Y
    Used by: Yell (+1 PA, Y=1), Accumulate (+1 PA, Y=1)

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x39: +SP_(Y) NS\x1b[0m
    Permanently increases target's Speed by Y
    Used by: Speed abilities (+1 Speed, Y=1)

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x3A: +Brave_(Y) NS\x1b[0m
    Permanently increases target's Brave by Y
    Used by: Brave-boosting abilities

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x3B: +Brave_(X) +PA/MA/SP_(Y) NS\x1b[0m
    Increases Brave by X AND PA/MA/SP by Y
    Dual stat boost in a single action

All stat boosts are 100% hit rate (no evasion/Faith check), NS (not Faith-
dependent), and apply to the caster or target depending on ability targeting.


\x1b[38;5;118m6B. STAT REDUCTIONS\x1b[0m
----------------------

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x1A: Hit_F(MA+Y)% -PA/MA/SP(X)\x1b[0m
    Reduces target's stat by X amount
    Hit rate = (MA + Y) * Faith scaling
    Which stat (PA/MA/SP) is determined by ability_id mapping

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x2B: Hit_(PA+Y)% -PA/MA/SP_(X)\x1b[0m
    Same as 0x1A but uses PA for hit rate instead of MA
    Used by: physical-based stat reduction

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x55: -PA_(Y) Hit_(MA+X)% NS\x1b[0m
    Reduces target's PA by Y
    Hit rate = MA + X (no Faith scaling)

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x56: -MA_(Y) Hit_(MA+X)% NS\x1b[0m
    Reduces target's MA by Y
    Hit rate = MA + X (no Faith scaling)


\x1b[38;5;118m6C. BRAVE/FAITH MODIFICATION\x1b[0m
------------------------------

\x1b[38;5;228mFormula 0x2A: Hit_(MA+X)% AffectBraveOrFaith(Y)\x1b[0m
    Changes target's Brave or Faith by Y amount
    Whether Brave or Faith is affected depends on ability_id
    Used by: Mediator abilities (Praise, Threaten, Preach, Solution)

\x1b[38;5;228mFormulas 0x61, 0x62: -Brave_(Y) Hit\x1b[0m
    0x61: -Brave_(Y) Hit_F(MA+X)% — Faith-scaled hit rate
    0x62: -Brave_(Y) Hit_(MA+X)% — No Faith scaling on hit
    Reduces target's Brave by Y
    Used by: Brave-reducing abilities

Note: Brave/Faith changes that accumulate beyond threshold values during battle
become permanent after battle (Brave changes of +/-4 or more per battle stick
permanently to the unit's base Brave/Faith).


================================================================================
\x1b[38;5;208m7. SONGS & DANCES\x1b[0m
================================================================================

Songs and Dances are continuous-effect abilities. The caster enters a
performing state and the effect triggers each CT tick while performing. The
caster cannot take other actions while performing. Effects are hardcoded by
ability_id, not by the X/Y parameters.


\x1b[38;5;118m7A. SONGS (Bard — Formula 0x1C)\x1b[0m
---------------------------------

Hit rate = X% per tick (NS — not Faith-scaled)
Target: All allies in range each tick

    Ability         ID    Y     Effect per Tick
    -------------   ---   ---   ------------------------------------
    AngelSong       86    20    Restores HP (amount = Y = 20)
    LifeSong        87    10    Restores MP (amount = Y = 10)
    CheerSong       88    -     +1 Speed
    BattleSong      89    -     +1 PA
    MagicSong       90    -     +1 MA
    NamelessSong    91    -     Random positive status (set 59)
    LastSong        92    -     Sets allies' CT to 100 (instant turn)

Songs stack over time — BattleSong applying +1 PA each tick for several ticks
produces a significant PA increase. Stat boosts from songs are permanent for
the duration of the battle.


\x1b[38;5;118m7B. DANCES (Dancer — Formula 0x1D)\x1b[0m
------------------------------------

Hit rate = X% per tick (NS — not Faith-scaled)
Target: All enemies in range each tick

    Ability         ID    Y     Effect per Tick
    -------------   ---   ---   ------------------------------------
    WitchHunt       93    20    Drains MP (amount = Y = 20)
    Wiznaibus       94    10    Drains HP (amount = Y = 10)
    SlowDance       95    -     -1 Speed
    PolkaPolka      96    -     -1 PA
    Disillusion     97    -     -1 MA
    NamelessDance   98    -     Random negative status (set 60)
    LastDance       99    -     Reduces enemies' CT to 0

Dances mirror Songs but target enemies with debuffs instead of allies with
buffs. The stat reductions from dances are permanent for the battle duration.


================================================================================
\x1b[38;5;208m8. SPECIAL MECHANICS\x1b[0m
================================================================================

These abilities have unique, hardcoded behavior that doesn't fit standard
damage/heal/status patterns.


\x1b[38;5;228mQuick (Formula 0x12)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Grants the target an immediate extra turn
    Mechanic:   Target's CT is set to allow an immediate action. The Quick'd
                unit acts before normal CT rotation resumes.
    Hit rate:   (MA + X) * Faith scaling
    Used by:    Quick (Time Mage)

\x1b[38;5;228mGolem (Formula 0x14)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Summons a barrier absorbing physical damage
    Mechanic:   Creates a pool of damage absorption for ALL allies. Physical
                attacks targeting any ally are absorbed by the Golem pool until
                it's depleted. Does not absorb magic damage.
    Hit rate:   (CasterFaith/100) * (MA + X)%
    Pool:       Based on caster's MA
    Used by:    Golem (Summon Magic)

\x1b[38;5;228mCT Reset (Formula 0x15)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Sets target's CT to 0
    Mechanic:   Target loses all accumulated CT, delaying their next turn
                significantly. Does not apply Stop status — CT continues to
                accumulate from 0 normally.
    Hit rate:   (MA + X) * Faith scaling
    Used by:    CT0 abilities

\x1b[38;5;228mLevel +1 (Formula 0x57)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Caster gains 1 level and applies status to self
    Mechanic:   Permanently increases caster's level by 1. Also applies
                inflict_status to caster (typically a buff). Level increase
                raises all stats according to level-up formulas.
    Hit rate:   100% (self-targeting)
    Used by:    Level-up abilities (monster)

\x1b[38;5;228mLevel -1 (Formula 0x59)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Target loses 1 level
    Mechanic:   Permanently decreases target's level by 1. Level decrease
                lowers all stats according to level-down formulas.
    Hit rate:   (MA + X) * Faith scaling
    Used by:    Level-down abilities (monster)

\x1b[38;5;228mMorbol (Formula 0x58)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Inflicts the Morbol compound status set
    Mechanic:   Applies a combination of debuffs (typically: Poison, Darkness,
                Confusion, Silence, Frog, Sleep, Don't Act, Don't Move)
    Hit rate:   (MA + X)%
    Used by:    Bad Breath (Morbol monster ability)

\x1b[38;5;228mDragon Series (Formulas 0x5A-0x5D)\x1b[0m
    Abilities that only affect Dragon-type units:
    0x5A: Hit 100% — applies status (Dragon-only targeting)
    0x5B: Heal Y% HP + apply status (Dragon HP restore)
    0x5C: +Brave(X) +PA/MA/SP(Y) — Dragon stat boost
    0x5D: Grants Quick to dragon — immediate extra turn

\x1b[38;5;228mOpposite-Sex (Formula 0x29)\x1b[0m
    Effect:     Charms target (only opposite-sex)
    Mechanic:   Hit check includes a gender comparison. Only succeeds if caster
                and target are opposite sexes. Genderless units are immune.
    Hit rate:   (MA + X)%
    Used by:    Charm (formula 0x29), certain reaction abilities
